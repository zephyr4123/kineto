# Fact sources for the narration

Checked line by line on 2026-10-04. Keep the qualifiers (大多, 大约, 通常, 讲究的) when editing: they are what keeps each line true.

| Claim | Notes | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Ripe coffee cherries are red | Most cultivars; yellow-fruited ones exist (Yellow Bourbon, Yellow Catuaí) | [CIRAD](https://www.cirad.fr/en/our-activities-our-impact/tropical-value-chains/coffee/plant-and-uses), [Daily Coffee News](https://dailycoffeenews.com/2019/04/18/the-fascinating-history-and-renewal-of-brazils-yellow-bourbon) |
| Afraid of frost and of heat, likes mild humid climates | Arabica optimum 18–21 °C annual mean; robusta 22–26 °C | [DaMatta & Ramalho 2006](https://www.scielo.br/j/bjpp/a/bDfpJwLr4xLcznSwy4b9zkf/?lang=en), [Carbon Brief](https://interactive.carbonbrief.org/commodity-profile-coffee/) |
| Mostly grown between the tropics ("coffee belt") | Some definitions widen it to 25°N–30°S; Baoshan, Yunnan is about 25°N | [Carbon Brief](https://interactive.carbonbrief.org/commodity-profile-coffee/), [CAS geodata](https://geodoi.ac.cn/gies_en/case2.aspx) |
| Usually two seeds per cherry | Peaberries are the exception | [CIRAD](https://www.cirad.fr/en/our-activities-our-impact/tropical-value-chains/coffee/plant-and-uses) |
| Uneven ripening; selective picking every 8–10 days | Large estates often strip-pick or machine-harvest, hence 讲究的 | [NCA](https://www.aboutcoffee.org/origins/lifecycle-of-coffee/), [Coffee Review](https://www.coffeereview.com/coffee-reference/from-crop-to-cup/growing-and-harvesting/ripeness-is-all/) |
| Natural vs washed processing; dried to about 11 % moisture | FAO: dry to 11 %, turn often; ICO export range 8–12.5 % | [FAO](https://www.fao.org/4/x6939e/x6939e11.htm), [ICO resolution 420](https://ico.org/wp-content/documents/iccres420e.pdf) |
| Hulled to pale green beans that smell grassy / hay-like | | [Pact Coffee](https://www.pactcoffee.com/blog/what-is-green-coffee), [Scheidig et al. 2007](https://portal.fis.leibniz-lsb.tum.de/en/publications/changes-in-key-odorants-of-raw-coffee-beans-during-storage-under--3/) |
| About 60 kg per bag, mostly by ship | 60 kg is the ICO standard and most common; Colombia 70 kg, much of Central America 69 kg | [Royal Coffee bag weights](https://cdn.royalcoffee.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/14100208/CoffeeWeights_FINAL.pdf) |
| About 200 °C for roughly ten minutes, green → yellow → brown | Typical 180–230 °C, 12–15 min; first crack at 400–600 s in Wilson's recordings | [Bicho et al. 2012](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/3959/395940113004.pdf), [Cropster](https://www.cropster.com/blog-post/back-to-basics-what-is-a-profile/) |
| First crack sounds like popcorn | | [Science News](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/coffee-beans-sing-distinct-tune) |
| Over a thousand aroma compounds, mostly formed during roasting | ~1000+ volatiles in roasted coffee vs ~200 in green | [Molecules 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9737409) |
| Several months from branch to cup | Latin American coffee typically 3–4 months to the roaster; some inland African origins 8–12 | [Coffee Review](https://www.coffeereview.com/green-coffee-freshness/), [Cafe Imports](https://www.cafeimports.com/europe/blog/?p=52964) |
